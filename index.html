OUR STORY

A focused food business with a simple purpose.

PNG Harvest exists to create a practical commercial bridge between growers in Papua New Guinea and consumers in Western Australia. We are starting small and deliberately focused: five traditional garden staples, one processing base in Port Moresby and one launch market in Perth.

Our aim is to add value in Papua New Guinea through sourcing, processing, packaging and employment, while giving Australian retailers access to convenient frozen products with a strong and authentic PNG origin.

FROM GARDEN TO FREEZER

A controlled process built around quality and traceability.

01

Source

Produce is received from approved growers and linked to a supplier lot.

02

Inspect

Crop identity, maturity, quality and contamination are checked.

03

Prepare

Produce is washed, peeled, cut and treated according to its product specification.

04

Pack

Finished product is weighed into 1 kg food-grade retail packs and sealed.

05

Freeze

Commercial freezing brings product to the required frozen condition, supported by batch records.

06

Deliver

The frozen cold chain continues through export, Australian clearance, storage and retailer delivery.

PNG GROWER NETWORK

Creating a reliable market for PNG-grown food.

PNG Harvest will begin with trusted family and community growers and progressively build a formal supplier network. The goal is consistent quality, fair commercial relationships and better visibility of future demand for participating growers.

PERTH WHOLESALE

Built for established retailers first.

PNG Harvest's first Australian sales channel will be wholesale supply to retailers in Perth. This keeps the launch model simple and allows production volumes to grow in line with genuine retailer reorders.

Wholesale pricing, launch dates and retailer onboarding will be released once product-specific import requirements and the first commercial trial shipment are finalised.
